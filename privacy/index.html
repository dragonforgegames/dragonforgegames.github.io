Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Developer: Dragon Forge Games

Contact: dragonforgegamestudio@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Dragon Forge Games handles information for the Android game Dragon Forge: Merge & Hatch. It is written for parents and players in plain language.

1. About the game

Dragon Forge is a dragon merge, idle and crafting game. You do not create a Dragon Forge account. Your dragons, items, settings, progress and offline Arena results are saved on your device. Arena opponents are computer-simulated. There is no online matchmaking and no Dragon Forge game server.

We do not receive a copy of your local save, and we do not ask for your name, location or contact details to play. Advertising and payment services may process information as explained below.

2. Local saves and information you send us

Dragon Forge is designed for a mixed audience that includes children under 13 and older players. The Android app shows a neutral age screen at first launch so protective advertising settings can be applied. Unknown or unanswered age is treated with child-protective advertising settings.

Local game data is used to run the game, remember your settings, calculate offline progress and keep purchase entitlements on your device. We do not upload your save to a Dragon Forge server.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include so we can reply. Please do not send payment-card details or unnecessary personal information.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are optional.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • IP address, which may be used for approximate location
  • App and ad interactions
  • Diagnostics used to keep ads working

Because the game includes children, ads for children and users of unknown age are configured for child-directed treatment, general-audience (G) ad content and Families Self-Certified ad SDKs. Personalized, interest-based ads and remarketing are not used for children or unknown-age users.

For eligible older users, personalized ads may be shown only where allowed and, where required, after consent. Non-personalized ads are not based on past behavior, but can still use limited information such as approximate location.

The No Ads purchase removes interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may still be available and can process data if you choose to use them.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Advertising ID and privacy choices

The app does not use the Android advertising ID for children or unknown-age users. For eligible older users, Android Settings usually let you reset or delete the advertising ID under Privacy or Google Ads settings. Menu names vary by device. Resetting this ID does not erase information Google already holds.

Where required, the app provides privacy choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can also email us with privacy questions.

5. EEA, UK and Switzerland consent

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s EU User Consent Policy applies. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform for required disclosures and choices about ads and related storage or access.

Children and under-age users are treated with protective settings and are not shown an adult personalized-ad consent request. Refusing personalized ads does not always stop every permitted ad-related process. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was already lawful.

6. Google Play purchases

In-app purchases, including No Ads and the Dragon Keeper Pass, are handled by Google Play Billing. Google handles your Google account and payment details under its own policies. We do not receive your full payment-card number or bank details.

Purchases are presented clearly so a parent or adult can understand that real money is involved. Google Play’s own purchase confirmation and parental controls may apply.

The app may receive product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state and entitlement information so it can deliver, acknowledge and restore purchases and help prevent fraud. There is no Dragon Forge purchase-validation server in this design.

Uninstalling the game does not erase Google’s purchase records, reverse a purchase or request a refund. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

7. Children and parental rights

Dragon Forge includes children under 13. We design the release for Families policy and COPPA-minded protections:

  • No Dragon Forge accounts
  • No Dragon Forge game servers
  • No Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics
  • Child-directed ad treatment for children and unknown age
  • No personalized or interest-based ads for children or unknown age

Parents and guardians may email dragonforgegamestudio@gmail.com to ask about information we control about their child, request deletion we can perform, or ask us to stop further collection or use where the law allows. We may need to verify parental authority. Clearing the app’s local data removes the on-device save. That does not automatically delete every record held by Google or other service providers.

8. Retention and deletion

Local saves remain on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Uninstalling deletes the local save on that device.

Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to respond and handle the request. Google’s retention rules apply to AdMob and Google Play Billing records. Contact us if you need help with a deletion request that involves those services.

9. Security

Local data is stored in the app’s normal Android storage. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK user-data transmissions use encrypted connections. No security measure is perfect, and providers may process information in more than one country under their own policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal or to complain to a privacy regulator. Email dragonforgegamestudio@gmail.com about data we control. For Google-held advertising or purchase records, we will explain how to use Google’s tools or help where we can.

In some US states, you may have rights related to sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. Child and unknown-age traffic is not used for personalized or interest-based ads or remarketing.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, providers or legal requirements change. We will revise the effective date. If a change needs extra notice or renewed consent, we will provide it.
